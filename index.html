Banking Bundle Cash Bonus

up to $1,000
cash
Open account Jul 3 – Oct 29, 2026 Preferred or Ultimate Package Paid ~9–10 months after opening

How to get it

  1. $700 — the chequing piece. Open a Preferred or Ultimate Package account during the offer window. Then, within 60 days of opening, set up at least one eligible automated recurring direct deposit (payroll or pension) that recurs monthly for a minimum of 6 consecutive months.
    — and also do any ONE of these within 60 days —
    • A recurring pre-authorized transaction of at least $50/month, continuing for 6+ consecutive months, or
    • One online bill payment of at least $50 through the mobile app or online banking, or
    • One eligible Visa Debit purchase with your ScotiaCard — online only, or in-store outside Canada
  2. +$200 — the savings piece. Within 60 days of opening the chequing account (and before Oct 29, 2026), open a Money Master Savings Account and enrol in Pay Yourself First or Savings Finder. Stay enrolled 90 consecutive days.

The trap: the direct deposit must genuinely recur monthly for six months. A single one-off transfer, or a deposit you push manually, will not satisfy "automated and recurring."

StartRight Program — Newcomers to Canada

up to $2,300
first-year value
Chequing Jul 3 – Oct 29, 2026 Credit card Aug 4 – Nov 1, 2026 No Canadian credit history required

How to get it

  1. Qualify as a newcomer. Permanent residents in Canada 0–5 years, international students, or foreign workers. Bring a PR card / Confirmation of PR or a work permit, plus a foreign passport or Canadian government photo ID.   2. Take the chequing bonus (up to $1,000). Chequing cash bonuses land roughly 9–10 months after account opening; credit card points within ~8 weeks of hitting each spend threshold.
Value math
Advertised "up to $2,300" is a rounded-down sum of ~$2,410 across program perks, the chequing bundle, and first-year card value — you will not receive it as cash.

Scotia High Interest Savings Account (HISA)

up to 5.00%
for 90 days
Promo 2.80% + base up to 2.20% First 90 days from opening Applies up to $5,000,000

How to get it

  1. Open your first Scotia HISA. You must not currently hold — and must not have held in the last 2 years — a Scotia HISA or a MomentumPLUS Savings Account.
  2. Understand the 5.00%. It is 2.80% promotional for 90 days stacked on the regular tiered rate. The top regular tier is 2.20%, so 5.00% only happens if you also hit that tier.
  3. Hold a Total Relationship Balance above $10,000 daily. Below $10,000 the regular rate is 0%, so you'd earn the 2.80% promo alone.
Read this as a 90-day rate, not an annual one. After the promotional period ends you keep only the tiered base rate: 0% under $10,000, rising to 2.20% at $500,000+.
Fine print
Start date
If the HISA is opened or switched into on a non-business day, the promotional period begins the first business day after.
Interest
Accrues daily, posts monthly. Account must remain in good standing for promotional interest to be paid.
Limit
One promotional offer per client, applied to the first eligible account opened.

How to read this sheet. Every figure, date and condition above was taken from Scotiabank's own public pages on August 18, 2026, and each card links to the page it came from. Summaries are condensed — before acting on any offer, open the source link and read the full legal terms, which govern.

Two patterns worth internalizing. First, nearly every credit card offer excludes anyone who has been a primary or secondary cardholder of any Scotiabank personal credit card in the past 2 years — a supplementary card on a partner's account counts and will disqualify you. Second, "up to $X" headline values bundle projected rewards, fee waivers and estimated savings alongside the actual bonus; the cash you can bank is always smaller than the headline.

Stacking. The chequing bundle, savings bonus and credit card bonus are designed to combine, and StartRight layers program perks on top of the same chequing mechanics. But the 2-year exclusions are shared across products — opening a no-fee card today can disqualify you from a $250 welcome bonus tomorrow. Sequence deliberately.
